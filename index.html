    LOOK UNDER THE HOODCalculations, reference values & source notes+

    The R calculations

    Conditional growth projection · M01
    P2 + (V12 / V11) × (starting RIT − P1)
    Conditional SD of growth · M02
    √(V22 − V12² / V11)
    Conditional growth index
    (observed growth − projection) / SD of growth
    Percentile approximation · M05
    100 / (1 + exp(−0.07056 × z³ − 1.5976 × z))

    For growth, z is CGI. For achievement, z is (score − national mean) / achievement SD. M05 is evaluated without rounding the input; displayed percentiles round positive ties upward and are limited to 1–99.

    Source and review limits

    This prototype uses pinned 2025 student-norm reference tables: 75,720 unique growth combinations and 1,848 achievement combinations. The calculator looks up the exact subject, grades and weeks; it does not substitute a nearby interval or refit a norms model.

    Mathematics and Reading grade 12 achievement mean, SD and percentiles are withheld pending source review. Valid growth results can remain available. Invalid asymmetric covariance rows remain excluded. Source/coefficient lineage questions and owner review remain open.
